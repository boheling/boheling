## Jing Liu

**Computational biologist turned AI-platform engineer for the life sciences.**

I build production AI that scientists trust — enterprise RAG, text-to-SQL database agents, agentic workflows, and the evaluation infrastructure that keeps them honest — shipped into high-stakes, regulated environments. Eight years in computational diagnostics (HLA typing, NGS/Nanopore, statistical inference) grounds the science; the last two-plus years building RAG, LoRA fine-tuning, and agentic platforms grounds the engineering.

Currently Bioinformatics & Software Lead at **Werfen**. Ph.D. in Molecular & Cellular Biology; published in *Cell*, *PNAS*, and *Molecular Microbiology*.
